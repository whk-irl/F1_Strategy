BEHIND THE TIMING

A race, from the data.

Car positions come from OpenF1's recorded location samples. Motion is interpolated between adjacent samples; this is an approximate circuit view, not lane-accurate positioning. Gaps and timing update at the provider's sample rate.

Telemetry, completed lap sectors, tyre stints, weather and race-control messages follow the replay cursor. A missing or stale sample displays as unavailable. Historical availability begins in 2023.

The strategy desk runs a nominal dry-race dynamic program. It is a planning reference, separate from the trained policy. The new fine-tuned policy did not beat the simulator's DP benchmark and has not been promoted.

Live sessions need an OpenF1 subscription and a server-side OPENF1_TOKEN. This app refreshes every 15 seconds with a 20-second playback buffer for smooth motion. It displays the current delay; provider and network latency still apply.
